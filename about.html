About HypeOutbid

HypeOutbid is India's first live bidding leaderboard for creators and founders.

Real bids. Real rankings. Real eyeballs.

What is this?

HypeOutbid is a paid visibility platform where creators, influencers, founders, and brands compete for ranking positions by placing bids. The highest bidder gets the #1 spot and maximum visibility. Every profile gets tracked clicks showing exactly how many people visited your Instagram.

How it works

  1. Place a bid — minimum ₹199. Pick your category.
  2. Get ranked — your position is determined by your bid amount.
  3. Get seen — thousands of visitors see the leaderboard. Your profile links directly to your Instagram.
  4. Get outbid? — someone pays more, you drop a spot. Bid again to reclaim your rank.

Why we built this

Instagram's algorithm decides who gets seen. We wanted to flip that. On HypeOutbid, visibility isn't decided by an algorithm — it's decided by how much you believe in yourself. Put your money where your mouth is.

Who built this?

Built by a team in India who wanted to give creators a transparent way to compete for attention. No gatekeepers. No algorithm. Just bids.
