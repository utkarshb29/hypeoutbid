Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

HypeOutbid.lol ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data We Collect

  • Instagram handle — displayed publicly on the leaderboard
  • Email address — for bid notifications and receipts
  • Payment info (UTR) — to verify your UPI payment
  • Website URL — optional, displayed on your profile card
  • Visit data — anonymous page views and click counts

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Display your profile on the public leaderboard
  • Send payment confirmations and outbid notifications
  • Track clicks on your profile (shown publicly as "views")
  • Improve our service

3. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your data. We share data only with:

  • Supabase (database hosting)
  • Razorpay / UPI (payment processing)
  • Vercel (website hosting)

4. Data Retention

Your profile remains on the leaderboard until you request removal. Email us to delete your profile and associated data.

5. Cookies

We use minimal cookies for functionality only. No third-party tracking cookies.

6. Your Rights

You can request data deletion, profile removal, or data export by emailing us.
